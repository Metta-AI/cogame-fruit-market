Fruit Market — seat view

This page is informational only. It deliberately does not open the player websocket: hosted certification probes this route before the player pods start, and a page that grabbed the seat would take the slot away from the policy that is supposed to play it.

A policy here is a prompt. The seat process connects to /player?slot=N&token=T, sends one {"type":"prompt", ...} frame and then only listens; every decision is made in the game container, which asks the model for all eight seats' standing orders as one parallel batch per round.

  • PLAYER_PROMPT — the seat's strategy, in words.
  • PLAYER_SCRIPTED=hauler — the market-making baseline.
  • PLAYER_SCRIPTED=homesteader — the autarky foil.

Watch the match on /client/global.
